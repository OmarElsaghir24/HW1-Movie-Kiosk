# Movie Theater Ticket Kiosk
- A customer can view available movies and showtimes.
- A customer can choose an available seat.
- A customer can purchase a ticket.

# Use Case: Purchase Ticket
- Primary Actor: Customer
- Precondition: The kiosk is operational, and at least one showtime is available.

- Main Steps:
  1. The customer views the available movies and showtimes.
  2. The customer selects the movie and showtime.
  3. The kiosk displays available seats for the selected showtime.
  4. The customer selects an available seat.
  5. The kiosk displays the ticket details and total price.
  6. The customer confirms the purchase and provides payment.
  7. The system processes the payment successfully.
  8. The system issues the ticket and marks the selected seat as booked for that showtime.
 
  - Postcondition: The purchase is recorded, the customer receives a ticket, and the selected seat is booked for the selected showtime.
